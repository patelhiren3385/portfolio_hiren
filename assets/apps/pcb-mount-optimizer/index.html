02 / MODEL–FE VERIFICATION

Check prediction error and engineering decisions.

A model is not accepted merely because its values look close. The app also checks whether model and direct FE agree on the strain, board-mode and component-dominance limits.

METHOD A

Model prediction

ROM, reconstruction, surrogate, analytical model or other fast prediction that you want to validate.

METHOD B

Direct FE result

Independent direct finite-element output for the same layout, constraints, load case and result definitions.
