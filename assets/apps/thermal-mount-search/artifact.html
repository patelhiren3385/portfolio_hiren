ABOUT THIS REPLAY

One mount per region. The thermal and membrane library of the free board is built once. Each region sweep is one bending offline stage (Algorithm 1, Steps 1 to 3 on the base of the mounts held during the sweep) followed by one online evaluation per candidate position: f1 from the zeros of the capacitance, the assembly zone strain from the capacitance at ω = 0, and the temperatures and thermal zone strains at the hot and the cold vertex from the library, all from small dense systems. The mount moves to the best position; the fields of that accepted layout are then shown for display only. The strip names the stage that is running and the time budget accumulates the measured seconds of each.
