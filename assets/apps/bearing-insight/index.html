What this model can—and cannot—say

A small CNN learns six simplified texture families: smooth, scoring, pits, corrosion-like coloration, smearing and crack-like lines.

It cannot establish oil starvation, misalignment, cavitation, serviceability or remaining life. Those conclusions require operating history, dimensions and further inspection.

There is no validated “unknown object” detector. Mixed damage may be forced into one class. Smooth appearance does not mean a bearing is healthy.

Path to a real bearing classifier

Collect licensed, expert-labelled photographs → group by physical bearing and acquisition session → reserve real-only validation and test sets → fine-tune a pretrained CNN → measure per-class recall and calibration → validate an unknown/mixed-damage rejection policy.

Use synthetic augmentation only within training, and retain it only if it improves untouched real-test performance. The present six-pattern experiment is not a substitute for this validation.
